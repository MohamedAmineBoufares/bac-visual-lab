# Code of Conduct

Bac Visual Lab is built for students, many of them under 18, together with teachers, translators and
developers. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions must keep it a welcoming
place.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and with people writing in their second language.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Accept that French, English and Arabic are all welcome here.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sexual content or attention of any kind. Never contact a minor privately.
- Sharing anyone's personal information without their consent.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, and may block anyone who breaks these rules. To report a
problem privately, use [GitHub's report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
option, or contact the maintainer listed in [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS). Reports are kept
confidential.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
